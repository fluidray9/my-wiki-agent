### Transformer Architecture (2026-04-21)
The Transformer architecture, introduced by Vaswani et al. in 2017, revolutionized NLP by replacing recurrence with self-attention mechanisms. Key derivatives include [[BERT]] and [[GPT]], both based on [[SelfAttention]].

Key contributors: [[AshishVaswani]], [[NoamShazeer]], [[LlionJones]]

The Transformer enabled the development of modern [[Large Language Model]]s.
