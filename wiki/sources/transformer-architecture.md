## Summary
The Transformer architecture was introduced in "Attention Is All You Need" (2017) by Vaswani et al. from Google Brain. It revolutionized natural language processing by replacing recurrence with self-attention mechanisms.

## Key Claims
- Transformer uses self-attention instead of recurrence
- Enables parallel processing of sequences
- Achieved state-of-the-art results on NLP benchmarks

## Key Quotes
> "This paper proposes a new simple network architecture, the Transformer, based solely on attention mechanisms." — Vaswani et al., 2017
