# Ashish Vaswani

Researcher at Google Brain, co-author of "Attention Is All You Need" (2017) which introduced the Transformer architecture.

## Key Contributions
- Lead author of the Transformer paper
- Proposed replacing recurrence with self-attention mechanisms
