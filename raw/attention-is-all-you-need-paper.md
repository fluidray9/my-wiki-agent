# Attention Is All You Need

The original "Attention Is All You Need" paper was published in 2017 by researchers from Google Research (not Google Brain). The paper introduced the Transformer architecture.

## Paper Details
- **Authors**: Ashish Vaswani, Noam Shazeer, Llion Jones, Niki Parmar, Jakob Uszkoreit, Llion Jones, etc.
- **Institution**: Google Research
- **Year**: 2017

## Key Points
- Introduced self-attention mechanism
- Replaced RNNs with attention mechanisms
- Enabled parallel processing of sequences

## Note on Contradiction
This paper states authors were from "Google Research" while other sources say "Google Brain". This contradiction should be flagged.
